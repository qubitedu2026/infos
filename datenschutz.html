Namen oder Kontaktdaten werden nicht abgefragt. Bitte tragen Sie keine personenbezogenen Daten in das Textfeld ein.

Rechtsgrundlage ist Ihre Einwilligung durch das Absenden (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO). Da keine Namen gespeichert werden, lässt sich ein Beitrag nachträglich in der Regel nicht mehr einer Person zuordnen.

3. Hosting via GitHub Pages

4. Programme data, site map and images (Google)

The schedule content is loaded directly from a Google spreadsheet when you open the page, and the site map and other images are loaded from Google Drive. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Your IP address is transmitted to Google in the process, and a transfer to the USA cannot be ruled out. Google participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. While the page is open, the programme data are reloaded about every five minutes so that changes become visible.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with § 3 DSG NRW. More information: Google privacy policy.

5. Feedback function

You can voluntarily send us feedback via the feedback field. Only the text you enter and the time of submission are stored, in a Google spreadsheet of the organising team (transmitted via Google Apps Script, provider as in section 4). Names and contact details are not requested. Please do not enter any personal data in the text field.

Feedback is used solely to improve the conference and is deleted no later than six months after the conference ends. Legal basis: your consent by submitting (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Since no names are stored, a submission can usually not be linked to a person afterwards.

6. Storage in your browser

The page stores in your browser's local storage (localStorage) which programme day you viewed last, so that it is shown again on your next visit. This information never leaves your device and can be deleted at any time via your browser settings. Legal basis: § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

7. Fonts

All fonts and scripts are loaded directly from this website. There is no connection to Google Fonts or any other external font or script provider.

8. Links to other websites

The page contains links, for example to Google Maps, to the legal notice of the I. Institute of Physics or to websites mentioned in abstracts. Data are only transmitted to these providers once you click a link. From then on, the provider's own privacy policy applies.
